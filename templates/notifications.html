How it works

Every attendee automatically receives a registration confirmation email (event name, date, time, venue, registration ID and QR code) the moment they register. A background scheduler also sends automatic reminder emails 3 days before, 1 day before, and on the day of their event — each attendee is reminded at most once per stage.
